Do this first if money just left your account

  • Call your bank or card issuer and ask them to stop or reverse the payment.
  • Stop replying to the scammer and do not send more money.
  • Change passwords on any account you shared, starting with email.
  • Keep screenshots, receipts and messages. You can add them to your report.

Simple steps

How reporting works

Five steps from your report to a clear plan. We cannot promise to recover money, but we will tell you honestly what your options are.

  1. File a report

    Tell us what happened and add any evidence you have.

  2. Case review

    A team member reads your report and checks the details.

  3. Follow-up

    We contact you if we need more information.

  4. Next steps

    We explain your options and which agencies or banks to contact.

  5. Updates

    You hear from us about the status of your report.

Our approach

Built for people who are stressed

Being scammed is not your fault. We keep the process short, the language plain and your information protected.

🛡

Private by design

Reports go straight to our review team. Other reporters can never see them.

💬

Plain language

No jargon. Short questions you can answer on a phone.

📝

Only what we need

We never ask for full card numbers, passwords or government ID numbers.

Common scam types

What kind of scam was it?

Not sure which one fits? That is fine. Choose the closest, or tell us in your own words.

📈

Investment scams

Fake trading, crypto or high-yield schemes.

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails, texts and websites that steal logins.

💼

Business email compromise

Fake invoices and payment-change requests.

❤

Romance scams

Someone online builds a relationship, then asks for money.

🎁

Lottery and prize scams

Fake winnings that require a fee to collect.

🔧

Tech support scams

Fake agents asking for remote access or payment.
