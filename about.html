About Civic Report Center

Civic Report Center helps people who have been targeted by scams or online fraud understand what happened and where to go next.

Who runs it

Civic Report Center is an official service run by the Federal Government of the UNITED STATES .

What we do

  • Read every report a person sends us.
  • Contact the person who reported, if we need more details or can help.
  • Explain their options and point them to the right places.

What we never do

  • We never charge a fee.
  • We never ask for passwords, full card numbers, PINs or remote access to your device.
  • We promise to recover lost money. full guarantees recovery of stolen funds.
